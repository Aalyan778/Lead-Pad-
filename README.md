## 🟢 𝐃𝐚𝐢𝐥𝐲 𝐮𝐬𝐞

### ① 📂 **𝐎𝐩𝐞𝐧 𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐏𝐚𝐝.**

The cursor is already in the big paste box at the top.

### ② 📋 **𝐂𝐨𝐩𝐲 𝐚 𝐥𝐞𝐚𝐝 𝐟𝐫𝐨𝐦 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐆𝐨𝐨𝐠𝐥𝐞 𝐌𝐚𝐩𝐬 𝐞𝐱𝐭𝐞𝐧𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐩𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐬 𝐂𝐭𝐫𝐥+𝐕**

in the paste box. You don't press Enter or do anything else.

### ③ 🔍 **𝐋𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐏𝐚𝐝 𝐜𝐡𝐞𝐜𝐤𝐬 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐥𝐞𝐚𝐝.**

It picks out the phone number, email, links (website, Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram) and the business name with its address. Then it compares them with every lead you have pasted before.

### ④ 📊 **𝐘𝐨𝐮 𝐬𝐞𝐞 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐮𝐥𝐭 𝐫𝐢𝐠𝐡𝐭 𝐮𝐧𝐝𝐞𝐫 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐩𝐚𝐬𝐭𝐞 𝐛𝐨𝐱:**

* 🆕 **New lead #12** means it is new. It gets the next serial number and goes to the **New (np)** tab.
* ❌ **Duplicate** means it was seen before. It goes to the **Duplicates** tab, with the reason (for example, "Phone match, already recorded as ABC Dental").

### ⑤ ⚡ **𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐬𝐜𝐫𝐞𝐞𝐧 𝐣𝐮𝐦𝐩𝐬 𝐛𝐚𝐜𝐤 𝐮𝐩 𝐭𝐨 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐩𝐚𝐬𝐭𝐞 𝐛𝐨𝐱,**

so you can paste the next lead straight away.

### ⑥ 🔁 **𝐑𝐞𝐩𝐞𝐚𝐭.**

The counter at the top shows your progress, for example 127 / 400. When you reach 400, the celebration popup appears. 🎉

## 📁 𝐔𝐬𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐚 𝐰𝐡𝐨𝐥𝐞 𝐟𝐢𝐥𝐞 𝐢𝐧𝐬𝐭𝐞𝐚𝐝

📄 Click **Check CSV file** and choose a file. LeadPad checks every lead in it at once and tells you how many are new and how many are duplicates.

---

## 🌙 𝐄𝐧𝐝 𝐨𝐟 𝐭𝐡𝐞 𝐝𝐚𝐲

### ① 💾 Click **Save .txt** or **Save Word**.

This saves only the new leads, numbered, with two blank lines after each one.

### ② 🗂️ Click **Save duplicates .txt** on the Duplicates tab if you want those too.

### ③ 🔄 Click **New day** to empty today's lists and start again from 1.

---

## 🧠 𝐇𝐨𝐰 𝐢𝐭 𝐫𝐞𝐦𝐞𝐦𝐛𝐞𝐫𝐬 𝐨𝐥𝐝 𝐥𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐬

Every new lead's phone, email, links and name+address are saved in a permanent "record" inside your browser. **New day** does not delete this record. That is how a lead from last week is still caught as a duplicate today. 🔒

---

## ⭐ 𝐎𝐧𝐞 𝐡𝐚𝐛𝐢𝐭 𝐭𝐨 𝐤𝐞𝐞𝐩

Once a week, click **Export record**. It saves a backup file called `all_leads.csv`. If you ever use another computer or browser, or clear your browser data, click **Import record** and choose that file to get your memory back. ♻️

---

## 💡 𝐐𝐮𝐢𝐜𝐤 𝐭𝐢𝐩𝐬

* ✏️ You can click any lead in the New tab to edit it, or press ✕ to delete it.
* 🎯 The **Goal** box at the top lets you change the 400 target.
* 📌 Always keep the LeadPad file in the same place and open it in the same browser, so your record stays with it.
